If you have wandered past the refrigerated pet section at the grocery store, you have probably eyed a roll of Freshpet and wondered whether it is actually better for your dog or just a pricier way to feed dinner. It is a fair question. Fresh food looks more like something you would eat yourself, and that appeal is exactly why the category has exploded.

Here is the honest answer: for most dogs, Freshpet is a solid, genuinely nutritious option — as long as you understand what you are buying and how to handle it. Let me walk you through what matters.

## Is Freshpet Actually Complete and Balanced?

This is the first question that matters, and the answer is reassuring. Freshpet recipes labeled "complete and balanced" are formulated to meet the nutrient profiles set by the [Association of American Feed Control Officials (AAFCO)](https://www.petmd.com/dog/nutrition/What-Is-AAFCO-and-What-Does-It-Do), the body that defines the minimum standards a dog food must hit to serve as a full diet.

**A food that meets AAFCO standards can be your dog's sole source of nutrition for life.** That is the whole point of the "complete and balanced" phrase — it means the recipe is not a topper or a treat, but a real meal that covers your dog's needs.

Freshpet formulates its recipes with veterinary and food-science oversight, and its foods are made with real meat as the first ingredient, gently steam-cooked, and preserved through refrigeration rather than artificial additives. If you want to understand the broader category before committing, our guide to [fresh dog food](/blog/fresh-dog-food) breaks down how it differs from everything else on the shelf.

One caveat worth repeating: always check the life-stage line on the package. A recipe formulated for "adult maintenance" is not the right pick for a growing puppy, who needs a "growth" or "all life stages" formula.

## What Fresh Food Does Well

Fresh, refrigerated food has a few real advantages over dry kibble, and they are not just marketing.

- **Minimal processing.** Kibble goes through high-heat extrusion that can degrade some nutrients. Fresh food is gently cooked, so it holds onto more of its original vitamins and moisture.
- **Higher moisture.** Dogs on dry-only diets get almost all their water from the bowl. Fresh food delivers meaningful hydration in the meal itself, which is easier on the kidneys and helps picky drinkers.
- **Digestibility and palatability.** According to the [American Kennel Club](https://www.akc.org/expert-advice/nutrition/fresh-raw-kibble-dog-food/), fresh diets are often more digestible and more appealing, which is why even fussy eaters tend to clean the bowl.

If your dog turns their nose up at kibble or struggles with a dry-only diet, these benefits are worth taking seriously. The same logic is why many owners mix in [wet dog food](/blog/wet-dog-food) for moisture and appeal.

## The Trade-Offs You Should Know

No food is perfect, and fresh food asks a little more of you than scooping kibble. Being honest about the downsides is the only way to decide if it fits your life.

- **It needs refrigeration.** Freshpet is not shelf-stable. Once opened, it should be covered, refrigerated, and used within about seven days.
- **Handling matters.** Treat it like the raw-adjacent food it is: wash the bowl right after each meal, wash your hands, and do not leave a full portion sitting out. Food left in the bowl too long can grow bacteria and turn.
- **Cost and convenience.** Fresh food generally costs more per serving than dry food and takes up fridge space. Kibble still wins on price, storage, and travel-friendliness.

None of these are dealbreakers, but they are the reason fresh food is a lifestyle choice, not just a nutrition upgrade. If budget is your main concern, a high-quality dry or wet food that meets AAFCO standards is a perfectly good path too.

## How to Decide If Freshpet Is Right for Your Dog

Ask yourself three practical questions:

1. **Does my dog need more moisture or better palatability?** If they are a reluctant eater or a low drinker, fresh food earns its keep.
2. **Can I handle the routine?** Fridge space, seven-day windows, and prompt bowl-washing are the real cost of admission.
3. **Does the specific recipe match my dog's life stage?** Confirm the AAFCO statement covers your dog's age.

For most healthy adult dogs, Freshpet is a genuinely good choice. If your dog has a medical condition — kidney disease, pancreatitis, food allergies — talk to your vet first, because those situations call for a tailored plan rather than a category swap. Our overview of [how much food to feed your dog](/blog/how-much-food-should-i-feed-my-dog) can help you get portions right whichever food you land on.

The best food is the complete-and-balanced one your dog thrives on and that you can afford to feed consistently. Freshpet clears the nutrition bar. Whether it clears the lifestyle bar is up to you.
